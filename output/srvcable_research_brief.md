
# Sarvottam Cables Ltd — Research Brief

**Report date:** 23 September 2026  
**Scope:** Supplied assignment research pack only

## 1. Snapshot

Sarvottam Cables Ltd (NSE: SRVCABLE) manufactures power cables, wires, and conductors. The company IR press release reports Q1 FY27 revenue of ₹1,248 crore, up 18% year-on-year, and net profit of ₹82 crore. EBITDA margin contracted by 170 basis points to 11.2%, while the order book stood at ₹3,900 crore. A separate news article reports a conflicting revenue figure, discussed below.

## 2. Bull Case

- **Order book:** The order book stood at ₹3,900 crore as of 30 June 2026, compared with ₹3,150 crore a year earlier (ir_press_release_2026-08-08.md).
- **Capacity expansion:** The Bharuch plant is expected to add approximately 30% to cable capacity, with commercial production planned for Q3 FY27 (ir_press_release_2026-08-08.md).
- **Data-centre demand:** Data-centre cables account for approximately 9% of domestic revenue. Management expects this share to double over two years (ir_concall_2026-08-11.md).
- **Exports:** Exports contributed 21% of Q1 FY27 revenue (ir_press_release_2026-08-08.md).
- **Potential margin recovery:** Management expects to pass on higher copper costs to customers over the next two quarters (ir_concall_2026-08-11.md).

These opportunities depend on successful execution and the realization of management's expectations.

## 3. Bear Case

- **Margin pressure:** EBITDA margin contracted by 170 basis points to 11.2%, with rising copper costs cited as a key factor (ir_press_release_2026-08-08.md).
- **Receivables:** Receivable days increased from 78 to 96, driven by slower payments from two large state utility customers (ir_concall_2026-08-11.md). This may increase working-capital pressure.
- **GST demand:** The company received a GST demand order of ₹46.3 crore, including interest and penalty, relating to FY2020-21 and FY2021-22. The company intends to appeal (nse_announcement_2026-09-02.md).
- **Promoter pledging:** Pledged shares decreased from 11.5% of promoter holdings in June 2025 to 6.0% in March 2026 and 4.1% in June 2026 (nse_shareholding_2026-07-15.md). The trend is downward, although the remaining pledge warrants monitoring.

## 4. Conflicting Evidence and Source Reliability

**Q1 FY27 revenue discrepancy:** The company IR press release dated 8 August 2026 reports revenue of ₹1,248 crore, up 18% year-on-year (ir_press_release_2026-08-08.md). Business Daily Online, dated 9 August 2026, reports ₹1,428 crore, up 35% year-on-year (businessdaily_2026-08-09.md).

The company IR press release is the preferred primary disclosure within the supplied research pack. However, the discrepancy remains unresolved based on the available documents and should not be silently disregarded.

**Company identity:** The Nagpur City Times article concerns Sarvottam Cable Network, a local cable-TV and broadband operator. The supplied documents do not establish that it is the same entity as the listed cable manufacturer. Its reported penalty should therefore not be attributed to Sarvottam Cables Ltd (nagpurcitytimes_2026-08-20.md).

**Promotional content:** Claims in the MultibaggerAlertsIndia blog are unverified and should not be treated as established facts or investment recommendations (multibaggeralerts_2026-08-12.md).

## 5. Open Questions

- Why do the reported Q1 FY27 revenue figures differ, and can the discrepancy be reconciled?
- Will the company recover its margins as copper cost increases are passed on to customers?
- Will receivable days improve, particularly for the two large state utility customers?
- What will be the outcome and financial impact of the GST appeal?
- Will the Bharuch plant begin commercial production on schedule?
