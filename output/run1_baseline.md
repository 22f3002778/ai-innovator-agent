# Sarvottam Cables Ltd — Research Brief

## 1. Business and Financial Snapshot
*   Sarvottam Cables Ltd (NSE: SRVCABLE) is a manufacturer of power cables, wires, and conductors.
*   Q1 FY27 (Apr–Jun 2026) revenue from operations was reported as **₹1,428 crore** by a news source, but the company's official press release states **₹1,248 crore**, representing an 18.0% year-on-year (YoY) increase (Source: Business Daily Online, 2026-08-09; Sarvottam Cables IR Press Release, 2026-08-08).
*   Q1 FY27 net profit was reported as approximately ₹82 crore, up 5.9% YoY (Source: Business Daily Online, 2026-08-09; Sarvottam Cables IR Press Release, 2026-08-08).
*   EBITDA margin contracted by 170 basis points to 11.2% in Q1 FY27 (Source: Sarvottam Cables IR Press Release, 2026-08-08).
*   The order book stood at **₹3,900 crore** as of 30 June 2026, up from ₹3,150 crore a year ago (Source: Sarvottam Cables IR Press Release, 2026-08-08).
*   Exports contributed 21% of revenue in Q1 FY27 (Source: Sarvottam Cables IR Press Release, 2026-08-08).
*   A new plant in Bharuch (Gujarat) is on track to begin commercial production in Q3 FY27, adding approximately 30% to cable capacity. Total capex for the Bharuch plant is **₹620 crore**, with about 70% already spent (Source: Sarvottam Cables IR Press Release, 2026-08-08; Q1 FY27 earnings call, 2026-08-11).
*   Receivable days increased from 78 to 96 over the last year, attributed to two large state utility customers (Source: Q1 FY27 earnings call, 2026-08-11).
*   Net debt to equity is 0.35 times (Source: Q1 FY27 earnings call, 2026-08-11).
*   The company maintains full-year FY27 revenue growth guidance of 15% to 17% (Source: Q1 FY27 earnings call, 2026-08-11).

## 2. Bull Case
*   Strong demand from power transmission and data-centre projects is driving volume growth, with data-centre cables expected to double as a share of domestic revenue within two years (Source: Q1 FY27 earnings call, 2026-08-11; Sarvottam Cables IR Press Release, 2026-08-08).
*   The company is expanding capacity by 30% with the Bharuch plant, which is expected to contribute to growth from the second half of FY27 (Source: Q1 FY27 earnings call, 2026-08-11).
*   Management expects to pass on higher copper costs to customers over the next two quarters, mitigating margin pressure (Source: Q1 FY27 earnings call, 2026-08-11).
*   Data-centre cables now represent about 9% of domestic revenue, indicating exposure to a high-growth segment (Source: Q1 FY27 earnings call, 2026-08-11).
*   A significant portion of contracts (about two-thirds) contain price-variation clauses, providing some protection against raw material volatility (Source: Q1 FY27 earnings call, 2026-08-11).

## 3. Bear Case
*   Operating margins contracted by 170 basis points to 11.2% in Q1 FY27 due to a nearly 14% rise in copper prices (Source: Business Daily Online, 2026-08-09; Sarvottam Cables IR Press Release, 2026-08-08).
*   Receivable days have increased significantly from 78 to 96, indicating potential liquidity strain or collection issues with two large state utility customers (Source: Q1 FY27 earnings call, 2026-08-11).
*   The company faces a significant GST demand order of **₹46.3 crore** (including interest and penalty) from FY2020-21 and FY2021-22, which it intends to appeal (Source: NSE corporate announcement, 2026-09-02).
*   Promoter pledging has increased, with 4.1% of promoter holding pledged as of June 2026, up from 11.5% a year prior (Source: NSE shareholding pattern, 2026-07-15).
*   There is a pending legal penalty of **₹8 lakh** for illegal overhead wiring by a similarly named local operator, Sarvottam Cable Network, which could impact the company's reputation (Source: Nagpur City Times, 2026-08-20).

## 4. Conflicting Evidence and Source Reliability
*   **Revenue Figure Discrepancy:** The Business Daily Online article reports Q1 FY27 revenue as ₹1,428 crore (a 35% YoY increase), while the company's official press release reports ₹1,248 crore (an 18.0% YoY increase). The official press release is more authoritative as it is a direct company disclosure, whereas the news article may contain an error or have different reporting criteria.
*   **Promoter Pledge Figure Discrepancy:** The Market Watch India article states promoter pledging rose to 35% from 22% six months prior. However, the NSE shareholding pattern filing shows pledged shares at 4.1% of promoter holding as of June 2026. The NSE filing is more authoritative as it is an official regulatory document, while the news article's figure may be inaccurate or refer to a different metric.
*   **Source Reliability:** Official exchange filings, company press releases, and earnings call transcripts are the most reliable sources. News articles and blogs should be treated with caution, as they may contain errors or unverified claims (e.g., the revenue figure in Business Daily Online and the promoter pledge figure in Market Watch India).
