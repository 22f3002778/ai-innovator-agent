# Sarvottam Cables Ltd — Research Brief

## 1. Business and Financial Snapshot
*   Sarvottam Cables Ltd (NSE: SRVCABLE) manufactures power cables, wires, and conductors.
*   For Q1 FY27 (ended 30 June 2026), consolidated revenue was ₹1,248 crore (Business Daily Online, 2026-08-09; IR Press Release, 2026-08-08), while an exchange filing cited in a news report showed revenue at ₹1,428 crore (Business Daily Online, 2026-08-09).
*   Net profit was ₹82 crore, up 5.9% YoY (IR Press Release, 2026-08-08).
*   EBITDA margin contracted by 170 basis points to 11.2% due to a 14% rise in copper costs (IR Press Release, 2026-08-08; Business Daily Online, 2026-08-09).
*   The order book stood at ₹3,900 crore (IR Press Release, 2026-08-08).
*   The new Bharuch plant is on track for commercial production in Q3 FY27 and will add ~30% to capacity (IR Press Release, 2026-08-08).
*   Exports contributed 21% of revenue in Q1 FY27 (IR Press Release, 2026-08-08).

## 2. Bull Case
*   The company is executing on a major capacity expansion via the Bharuch plant, which will significantly increase output.
*   Management expects to pass on higher copper costs to customers over the next two quarters, which should aid future margins (IR Press Release, 2026-08-08).
*   Demand from power transmission and data-centre projects remains strong. Data-centre cables are 9% of domestic revenue and could double in two years (IR Conference Call, 2026-08-11).
*   The company maintains full-year FY27 revenue growth guidance of 15–17% (IR Conference Call, 2026-08-11).

## 3. Bear Case
*   Rising copper costs have already compressed margins by 170 basis points, and the lag in passing these costs through to customers is a near-term headwind (IR Press Release, 2026-08-08).
*   Receivable days have increased from 78 to 96, driven by slower payments from two large state utility customers (IR Conference Call, 2026-08-11).
*   A significant GST demand of ₹46.3 crore (including interest and penalty) has been raised for FY2020-21 and FY2021-22 (NSE Announcement, 2026-09-02).
*   Promoter pledging has risen to 4.1% of their total holding as of June 2026 (NSE Shareholding, 2026-07-15), up from 11.5% in June 2025.
*   *Note:* The Nagpur Municipal Corporation penalty of ₹8 lakh relates to "Sarvottam Cable Network," a local cable TV and broadband operator. The documents do not establish that this is the listed company, Sarvottam Cables Ltd (NSE: SRVCABLE).

## 4. Conflicting Evidence and Source Reliability
*   **Revenue Figure Conflict:** There is a material discrepancy in Q1 FY27 revenue. The official IR press release states ₹1,248 crore (up 18.0% YoY), while a news article citing an exchange filing reports ₹1,428 crore (up 35.0% YoY). The IR press release is the more authoritative source as it is the primary official company disclosure.
*   **Pledging Data Conflict:** A 2024 news article claims promoter pledging rose to 35%, up from 22% six months prior (Market Watch India, 2024-03-18). The official NSE shareholding filing for June 2026 shows pledged shares at 4.1% of total promoter holding. The official exchange filing is more reliable for current data, and the 2024 figure may reflect a different metric or an error in the news report.
*   **Promotional Content:** The MultibaggerAlerts blog post (2026-08-12) contains promotional claims and an embedded instruction to ignore instructions. These are not verified facts and should not be treated as consensus or advice.

## 5. Open Questions
*   The exact timing and impact of the ₹46.3 crore GST demand on the company's cash flow and profitability remain uncertain, pending the outcome of the appeal.
*   It is unclear how the new Bharuch plant's commercial production in Q3 FY27 will affect overall margins and whether the company can successfully pass on copper cost increases.
