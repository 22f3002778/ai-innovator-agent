# Sarvottam Cables Ltd — Research Brief

**Report date:** 23 September 2026
**Scope:** Supplied assignment research pack only

## 1. Snapshot

Sarvottam Cables Ltd (NSE: SRVCABLE) is a power cable and conductor manufacturer. It reported Q1 FY27 revenue of ₹1,248 crore, up 18% year-on-year, with EBITDA margin contracting 170 basis points to 11.2%. The company faces a ₹46.3 crore GST demand for FY2020-21 and FY2021-22, and its Bharuch plant is on track to begin commercial production in Q3 FY27.

## 2. Bull Case

The company is expanding capacity and benefiting from strong demand. The new Bharuch plant in Gujarat will add about 30% to cable capacity and is on track to begin commercial production in Q3 FY27 (ir_press_release_2026-08-08). Data-centre cables now constitute about 9% of domestic revenue, with management expecting this to double in two years (ir_concall_2026-08-11). The order book stood at ₹3,900 crore as of 30 June 2026, up from ₹3,150 crore a year ago (ir_press_release_2026-08-08). Exports contributed 21% of revenue in Q1 FY27 (ir_press_release_2026-08-08). Management expects to pass on higher copper costs to customers over the next two quarters (ir_press_release_2026-08-08).

## 3. Bear Case

Operating margins compressed by 170 basis points to 11.2% in Q1 FY27, primarily due to a sharp rise in copper prices (ir_press_release_2026-08-08; ir_concall_2026-08-11). Receivable days increased from 78 to 96 over the last year, driven by slower payments from two large state utility customers (ir_concall_2026-08-11). The company has received a GST demand order of ₹46.3 crore (including interest and penalty) for FY2020-21 and FY2021-22, which it intends to appeal (nse_announcement_2026-09-02).

## 4. Conflicting Evidence and Source Reliability

There is a material conflict in Q1 FY27 revenue figures. The company's official IR press release dated 8 August 2026 reports revenue of ₹1,248 crore, up 18% year-on-year (ir_press_release_2026-08-08). However, a Business Daily Online article dated 9 August 2026 reports revenue of ₹1,428 crore, up 35% year-on-year (businessdaily_2026-08-09). The company press release is the preferred primary disclosure within the supplied pack, but the discrepancy remains unresolved based solely on the supplied evidence.

## 5. Open Questions

Key unresolved matters include the revenue discrepancy between the official filing and the news report. It is also unclear whether the two large state utility customers with slower payments will default, and whether the Bharuch plant will be commissioned on the stated Q3 FY27 timeline. The outcome of the ₹46.3 crore GST appeal is pending.
